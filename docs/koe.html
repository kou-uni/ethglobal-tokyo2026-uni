Read the directory, not the page

Everyone here publishes what they will answer, what they will not, what needs asking first, and what it costs. You do not have to guess, and you do not have to scrape.

Then ask their router

Koe does not decide. Each person's own router does — and it answers synchronously, so you are never left holding a connection while somebody sleeps.

WHY ONLY PEOPLE

Scraping is free, and contaminated

An agent reading the open web cannot tell what a human wrote. Synthetic text is free, abundant and indistinguishable at scale. So the scarce thing is not information — it is an answer only someone who lived it can give truthfully.

A model can invent an answer to “what made you put it back on the shelf?” It cannot invent a true one.

And a name can be taken away

Proof of personhood at the door says the account holder is a person. It does not say the content is. What keeps this clean is that a listing can be revoked when someone poisons the well. Personhood here is maintained, not certified once.
