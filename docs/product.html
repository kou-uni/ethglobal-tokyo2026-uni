TRY IT — ONE LINK, NO SETUP

An agent is researching. It needs a person.

Open it on your phone. An agent asks you something it cannot answer without you — “tell me about a time it let you down”. Press Yes and you prove you are a person at that moment; the payment settles to the wallet you named, so you check your own balance, not our word for it.

Press No, or just close the tab, and the next screen names each thing that did not happen. No wallet? There is a skip button — the decision is the part worth seeing.

Start — an agent is asking →
  1. You say where to pay you. Our buyer wallet signs a payment to that address. The amount and the destination are inside the signature — nothing downstream can raise either, not the facilitator and not us.
  2. A question arrives with exactly five fields — who, what, purpose, price, deadline. Ten ordered rules decide; the first match wins. A model can invent an answer to it. It cannot invent a true one — which is the whole reason there is money here.
  3. It was not settled by a rule, so it waits for a person — bundled, ranked, and held to two a day, a cap she set.
  4. Yes asks her to prove personhood now, not at signup. The screen shows how many seconds ago — a signed claim from the issuer, checked against this server's clock.
  5. Some never reach you at all. An agent asking for your home address or your wallet is refused by a rule you set — and you can go and look at what it wanted, with the rule named beside it. See the drop list → A refusal nobody can inspect is the same as never having been asked.
  6. Doing nothing is an answer. The deadline passes and the request is denied. The payment authorization expires with it, so nobody can settle it, including us.
The buyer's wallet is ours and it is rate-limited. This is open on the internet with no login, so the number of payments it will sign in an hour is capped. Past the ceiling the flow still works — it just stops paying, and says so.
