WHERE THIS GOES — AN AGENT'S FINANCES

An agent that works around the clock spends and earns around the clock. The question that decides everything is not how much — it is where a person still has to be involved. Which is the half-step before execution, and the part nobody has built.
1
One seller A morning screen: what an agent moved overnight, and the two things that needed a person. That is today.
2
A team The same shape, more people. The cap stops being one person's tolerance and becomes a policy — who may be interrupted, for what, how often.
3
A company's agent treasury Agents moving money continuously. The layer that decides which movements a human must see at all — and holds the evidence that a person consented, which is where liability actually lands.
We did not build the execution layer, and we are not claiming it. Wallets, treasury, custody and execution all have owners. Yohaku is the half-step before them: deciding whether money should move means deciding, first, whether to ask. Spain's data protection authority said in February 2026 that an agent's autonomy creates no new legal category and the deploying organisation keeps full responsibility — so whoever holds the record of consent holds the part that matters.
